  - E5 table definition: `B2_model_comparison.csv` has one row per arm (three base models, weighted average, stacking).   - E5 ensemble protocol: both arms are cross-fitted. The weighted average uses non-negative weights on the simplex, fitted on the other folds' OOF rows by a deterministic 0.05-step grid that minimizes log loss; the stacking arm is a logistic meta-learner on the base models' OOF probabilities, also fitted on the other folds' OOF rows. Each fold's prediction therefore comes from a blend that never saw that fold. The weights refitted on all rows are recorded in `E5_ensembles.json` for the report but are not used for scoring. Rejected: fitting the weights on the same OOF rows that are scored (optimistic) and equal/inverse-variance weights (no fitting at all).

The arm is correct, the objective is the wrong question for this trio.
